Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game work offline?
Yes. Golden Flux is fully playable offline. Your factory keeps producing while you are away and progress is saved on your device.

How is my progress saved?
Progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all saved data.

How do I set my avatar?
Open the Profile screen and choose a photo from your gallery or take one with the camera.
